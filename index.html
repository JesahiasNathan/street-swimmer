Race the road

Street Swimmer is an arcade racing game for iPhone and iPad. Drive an endless road through traffic, race rivals for prize money, dodge the police, tune your cars in the workshop, and race your friends online.

  • 186 cars to collect, from hot hatches to hypercars
  • Races with power-ups, jumps, skyways and road works
  • Online races with friends, text and voice chat
  • A workshop to service and upgrade every car
